# B2B apps and APIs overview

- Welcome to our training on B2B apps and APIs.
 Amazon Business provides a suite of features
 that help optimize business to business or B2B sales.
 These include apps that can help you manage
 and automate your B2B operations with features
 that help with pricing and discounts,
 offer product opportunities and delivery options.
 Apps are software solutions that connect
 with your Seller Central account,
 allowing you to perform various tasks
 throughout the selling life cycle.
 APIs or application program interfaces
 are a set of rules and protocols
 that allow different software applications
 to communicate and interact with each other.
 APIs act as point of contact between a client and a server
 with the client sending requests
 and the server sending responses.
 API documentation contains information
 on how developers should structure these requests
 and responses.
 APIs are used to build apps.
 For example, your weather app utilizes an API
 that communicates with the Weather Bureau's software system
 to show you daily weather data.
 You have two options for adopting APIs as a seller.
 You can use existing public apps with APIs
 developed by third party integrators or three PIs
 or you can build your own app using our API.
 Making your own app may involve hiring a developer
 if you do not already have one on your team
 in order to create a custom app
 to meet your business's needs.
 Private apps are built just for your Seller account
 so only you can use them.
 To develop your own app, go to apps and services
 in the Seller Central main menu and click develop apps.
 Next, click on the add new app client button,
 then enter your app name and choose API type as SP API.
 Finally, choose the right roles for your developer profile
 and hit save and exit to get the needed data privileges
 for your application.
 For more information on how to assign roles to a developer
 and how to integrate a private app,
 watch our video series about selling partner apps
 starting with SP API overview on Seller University.
 If you intend to use an app across various e-commerce channels
 and do not want to build your own,
 you can use a public app.
 To adopt an existing app,
 go to the selling partner app store in Seller Central.
 There you'll find a list of public apps
 to meet your business's needs
 when you filter by Amazon business.
 While you can browse and search all the apps we carry
 in the selling partner app store,
 the best way to view the list of B2B apps
 is on the page Amazon Business APIs.
 Select B2B from the Seller Central main menu
 and click B2B Central.
 From here, you'll find B2B API overview in the menu.
 Scroll to the bottom of that page
 to see the third-party integrators
 that have adopted an Amazon business or AB feature.
 This section illustrates the AB features included
 with each third-party integrated app.
 The three PIs are organized by the features available,
 like business prices and quantity discounts,
 pricing, delivery experience,
 multi-location inventory, and package hierarchy.
 You can search the heading for the features you're looking for.
 If you're looking for an app that includes pricing
 or reports and analytics, for instance,
 look for three PIs in those sections.
 After you identify your selections,
 click the name of the three PIs to find out more about it.
 Each has a detailed page where you can review its features,
 read a description to learn about the intended users,
 its pricing, and reviews.
 On the right-side menu, you'll find options under functionality
 and supported languages.
 Note, while we work to keep our information accurate,
 click Visit Developer website to go directly to their page
 for the most up-to-date information about the app.
 Select Authorize Now to start the process
 of authorizing the three PIs to access your seller data.
 Don't forget to mark the checkbox.
 I direct Amazon to provide the three PIs access
 to my selling partner account and related data.
 I am responsible for any actions taken by the application.
 Then, click Sign Up to reach the login page.
 To manage your apps once authorized,
 go to the Seller Central Main menu
 and select Apps and Services.
 Then, click Manage Your Apps.
 From this page, you can see the list of developers
 you've authorized, including their name, ID, app name,
 status, authorization date, and MWS authorization token.
 Use the Actions column to enable authorization,
 reauthorize, disable authorization,
 or opt-out of notification.
 Thank you, and happy selling in the Amazon store.
