# 3.1 Apply to sell a restricted product, category, or brand

Welcome to our training on how to apply
 to sell a restricted product category or brand.
 There are a few steps you should take
 before listing a product in the Amazon store.
 First, confirm that it's okay to sell.
 Make sure it's safe and complies
 with all laws and regulations.
 You should also review Amazon's restricted product
 and product compliance policies
 to verify that it isn't prohibited by Amazon
 and meets all product-specific requirements.
 Next, determine if the product requires any approvals.
 Although products can be listed
 in the Amazon store without approval,
 we require approval to list certain products,
 categories, and brands enrolled in Amazon brand registry.
 You can obtain approval to sell a product, category,
 or a registered brand by completing
 a selling application in Seller Central.
 An application may include document requests,
 performance checks, and other qualifications.
 It's designed to help ensure that customers can shop
 with confidence in the Amazon store,
 knowing that products are safe and authentic.
 How will you know if your product requires an approval?
 We've built approval checks and applications
 right into the listing process.
 To list a product that's already sold in the Amazon store,
 open the Seller Central main menu,
 hover over Catalog, and click Add Products.
 Search for your product using a product ID.
 You can also search by using a GTIN, like UPC, ISBN,
 EAN, or JAN.
 Or search using an ASIN, or other ID,
 paired with descriptive information,
 like brand or product name.
 Next, locate the product you want to sell
 in the search results.
 If it requires approval,
 you'll see a Show Limitations link,
 which you can click to get a summary
 of listing requirements.
 Review the requirements and select a product condition
 using the drop-down menu.
 Click the Apply to Sell button,
 and then the button on the Selling Application page
 to begin the approval process.
 - The specific selling application you'll need to complete
 will depend on the product you're trying to list
 and the type of approval you need to secure.
 Let's review a selling application for a category first,
 then a Selling Application for a registered brand.
 On the Selling Application for category page,
 start by answering any product-related questions,
 then review the list of requirements
 for the purchase invoice you'll need to upload.
 Check each box after you verify that your document
 meets that requirement.
 The purchase invoice you submit must be dated
 within 180 days and include your name and address,
 matching the information in your selling account.
 It must also include the name and address
 of the product's manufacturer or distributor,
 and show a combined purchase of at least 10 units.
 That means the invoice should show a paid balance
 for at least 10 units,
 demonstrating that you own the inventory.
 If you're omitting the pricing for individual items
 on your invoice, check the box to confirm.
 Then check the box acknowledging that we may verify
 your document by contacting the manufacturer
 or distributor it identifies.
 After checking each required box,
 upload your purchase invoice.
 Make sure it's a high-resolution PDF file
 in the correct orientation.
 Then provide any optional comment
 that might help us assess your application,
 along with contact information we can use
 to ask follow-up questions.
 Select Submit to complete the application.
 - If you need to complete a selling application
 for a brand enrolled in brand registry,
 you'll follow a similar process.
 - On the Selling application for brand page,
 select the document type you want to upload
 as part of your application.
 Either a purchase invoice for products from the brand
 that you received from a manufacturer or distributor,
 or a letter directly from the brand
 authorizing you to sell their products.
 For a purchase invoice, you'll need to submit a document
 that meets the same requirements we reviewed
 for a category approval.
 For an authorization letter,
 you'll need to submit a document that is dated
 within 180 days and includes your name and address,
 matching the information in your selling account.
 Your letter also needs to be on the brand's letterhead
 and include the name and address of the brand.
 Make sure it's a high-resolution PDF file
 in the correct orientation.
 After uploading either document,
 you'll provide contact information,
 plus any optional comments.
 Then select Submit to complete the application.
 When you submit a selling application for a brand
 enrolled in brand registry,
 you're requesting approval to match offers
 to the brand's existing product detail pages.
 This approval doesn't provide you
 with additional listing privileges
 to create or edit product detail pages for the brand,
 or access to brand selling benefits like stores
 and A+ content.
 Those privileges and benefits are reserved for sellers
 internal to the brand and authorized third-party resellers.
 If you'd like more information
 about requesting additional listing privileges
 and brand selling benefits,
 see our Seller University video,
 Request Access to Brand Selling Benefits.
 Now that you know how to request an approval
 for a product that's already sold in the Amazon store,
 let's review how to request approval for a new product.
 You'll start the process the same way,
 double checking that there's no existing listing
 for your item.
 Search for the product using the Product Adding tool.
 If it doesn't appear in the search results,
 click the Create a New Listing button.
 During the listing creation process,
 you'll find out if your new product
 requires a category approval when you select a product type.
 If it does, you'll see Learn More Text
 and a Lock icon next to that category.
 Click the text to open the categories and products
 that require approval help page.
 Then find and click the Request Approval link.
 Next to the relevant product category.
 This will bring you to the Selling Application
 for category page, which you can complete
 using the same steps we reviewed earlier.
 You'll find out if you need to complete
 a selling application for a registered brand
 a little later in the listing creation process
 while entering information for the new product detail page.
 After entering or selecting the brand's name
 in the brand name field, you'll receive error 5461.
 Use the link in the error message
 to access the Selling Application
 for Catalog Authorization page
 where you'll request approval to list and sell
 the item for the brand.
 As part of the application,
 you'll provide a letter of authorization from the brand
 as well as product information and images
 to create the new ASIN and product detail page.
 Note that this is the only scenario
 in which a seller other than the brand owner
 or another seller granted listing privileges
 for the brand can apply to create a new listing
 for a brand enrolled in brand registry.
 After completing a selling application for a product,
 category or brand, you'll receive confirmation
 within 24 hours that we're reviewing your approval request.
 In most cases, that review process
 is completed within three business days.
 To monitor the status of a selling application,
 open the Seller Central Main Menu,
 hover over Catalog and click View Selling Applications.
 After the status of an application is approved,
 select List Products to start adding
 that product to your inventory.
 For more tips on submitting a selling application,
 review the picture requirements for when you apply to sell
 and invoice requirements for when you apply to sell
 videos in Seller University.
 This concludes our training on how to apply to sell products,
 categories or brands that require approval to sell.
 Continue to the next video in the series to learn our tips
 for complying with Amazon's product detail page policies
 or resolving violations.
 Thank you and happy selling in the Amazon Store.
