# Five key Sponsored Products reports for campaign optimization

Want to get more from your sponsored products ads?
 These five key reports will help you analyze performance and optimize your campaigns for better results.
 After watching this video, you'll be able to
 locate key sponsored products reports,
 interpret campaign performance metrics,
 use report data to optimize campaigns,
 and make data-driven advertising decisions.
 In Seller Central, select Advertising, then select Reports under Measurement and Reporting.
 Let's start with the Advertised Product Report.
 This report shows performance metrics for individual products across campaigns,
 helping track clicks and sales attributed to advertising.
 Use this report to compare how products perform with advertising and prioritize the ones showing the strongest return.
 Next, we'll examine the Campaign Report.
 This gives you an overview of activity across your sponsored products campaigns for a specific time period.
 You can view metrics in summary, hourly, daily, weekly, monthly, or quarterly formats.
 Here, you'll see bidding strategy, budget, and sales.
 Use the Campaign Report to get better insights across different placement types
 and identify campaigns that could benefit from a bid adjustment.
 Now, let's move on to the Search Term Report.
 This report reveals what shopping queries are leading to ad clicks.
 Use this report weekly to identify high-performing search terms
 and to exclude underperforming ones through negative targeting.
 You can edit existing campaigns in the Campaign Manager
 or create new campaigns using these keywords.
 The fourth report we'll cover is the Placement Report,
 which shows where your ads are appearing, top of search, product pages, and more.
 Look at performance by placement type to make informed bidding decisions
 and boost visibility in your highest-value placements.
 Access placement bid adjustments in the Campaign Manager under the Bid Adjustments tab
 or in the Bid Adjustments card when creating a campaign.
 Finally, we'll explore the Budget Report.
 This tracks how your campaigns use allocated spend.
 It shows estimated missed opportunities and average time in budget.
 Use it to identify when campaigns run out of budget too soon
 and adjust your spend to keep top-performing campaigns running.
 These reports give you the information needed to continuously refine your campaigns.
 Monitor metrics like return on ad spend,
 ROAS, which measures your ad sales divided by ad spend,
 ad attributed sales, and time in budget regularly
 to stay aligned with your goals.
 View reports in the Advertising Console.
