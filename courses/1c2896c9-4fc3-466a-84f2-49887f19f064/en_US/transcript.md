# How to add a shoppable image to your Store

Shoppable images in your store help customers see your products in context, engage with their
 details, and add them directly to their carts. If you're using the product highlight or
 marquee template, or if you've added a split width section to your page, you can see empty
 tiles ready for you. Click on a tile and find Shoppable Image. Upload a lifestyle image
 showing at least one product, then add the products featured. You can adjust where
 they're active on the image and add more, up to six products. Click Save. You can
 also add a shoppable Image as the only full width tile in a section. Add a new
 section and your shoppable Image in the same way.
