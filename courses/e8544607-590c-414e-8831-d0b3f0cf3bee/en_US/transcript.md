# List products without a Product ID (UPC/GTIN Exemption)

A product ID such as GTIN, UPC, EAN, JAN, or ISBN, helps to provide fast and accurate product identification,
 streamlines inventory management, and reduces errors through barcode scanning. However, some
 products do not require a product ID in order to be sold in the Amazon store. To list a
 product without a product ID, you'll need to apply for a GTIN exemption during the listing process.
 To determine if your product is eligible for GTIN exemption, start by making sure it is not
 already being sold in the Amazon catalog. It does not have a product ID or GS1 barcode and that you
 have approval to sell in that product's category if required. Next, check to make sure that your
 product falls into one of the categories that allows GTIN exemptions. These include private label
 or handmade products, products that you manufacture, brand, or publish without barcodes,
 parts without a product ID, for example auto parts or mobile accessories, bundled products,
 for example a belt or wallet pack, or generic unbranded products. To start your application,
 go to the Add Products page in Seller Central and enter your product title into the search bar.
 At the bottom of the search results, click Create a New Listing. Start by filling in your
 product details and select the appropriate product type and category. Then, enter a brand name for
 your product. If your product is generic, select this product does not have a brand name. Check
 this product does not have a product ID and click Next. If you are a brand owner or are selling
 generic products, you'll see an auto approval pop up. Your approval could take up to one hour to
 take effect, so feel free to save your listing as a draft and come back later. If you try to list
 a product from a brand that requires a product ID or if your product is a book and you check this
 product does not have a brand name, your GTIN exemption will be automatically declined. If you
 are selling a brand that does not require a product ID, you'll be prompted to apply for a GTIN
 exemption. You might see multiple approval required alerts during this process. However,
 all approvals will be processed through a single application. Make sure your application
 includes clear, high-quality, real-world photos of all sides of your product to reduce the chances
 of your application being rejected. Once your application has been submitted, it can take up to
 48 hours to receive a decision. You can check the status of your applications on the View Selling
 Applications page. You'll need to request separate GTIN exemption approvals for each product category
 and each Amazon store where you intend to sell. Once approved for a category in a store,
 you can list multiple products within that category without additional exemptions.
 To learn more about specific requirements for your product category or troubleshoot common
 errors like brand name mismatches, go to the list products that do not have a product ID
 help page in Seller Central.
