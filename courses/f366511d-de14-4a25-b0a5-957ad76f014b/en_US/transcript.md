# Why top sellers rank each ASIN before scaling

Hey, I'm Michael Koka, Head of Digital Commerce at Revolution Nutrition. I scaled our Amazon channel
 from zero to tens of millions in annual revenue in just two years. Everything I'm sharing comes
 from real execution. Let's dive in. Today I want to talk about what actually drives
 sustainable growth on Amazon. One of the biggest lessons sellers learned is that
 traffic alone doesn't scale a business. Systems do. Start by nailing operations early.
 Make sure fulfillment, inventory planning and forecasting are solid before pushing aggressive
 marketing. Stockouts kill momentum and hurt a ranking, so availability really matters more
 than most people realize. Approach advertising with purpose, don't just spend, structure campaigns
 around your product life cycle, launch, ranking, then profitability. Separate branded and non-branded
 campaigns optimize using real search key terms and consistently cut wasted spend. Another major growth
 lever is repeat customers. Think about bundles, promotions, subscribe and save and your overall
 post purchase experience. Increasing lifetime value makes scaling far more sustainable than
 chasing one-time buyers. Finally, expand strategically. Don't launch everything at once.
 Scale asin by asin so each product gets proper attention, inventory support and optimization.
 Real growth on Amazon comes from consistency. Small weekly improvements across operations,
 advertising and customer experience compound faster than most sellers expect.
