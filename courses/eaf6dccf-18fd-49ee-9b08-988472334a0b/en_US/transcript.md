# Sell in the Amazon store: 5-minute overview for beginners

Selling on Amazon can be broken down into four steps. Step 1. Create an Amazon Seller Central account on sell.amazon.com. Step 2. List Products for Sale. Step 3. Fulfill Customer Orders and Provide Outstanding Customer Service. Step 4. Receive payments from your sales proceeds and maintain your account health. Let's go over these four steps.
 Step 1. Create an Amazon Seller Central Account. Before you register for an account with Amazon,
 choose between professional or individual selling plan. Confused on which plan is right for your business?
 There are a few important differences. Between the two plans. Let's compare. Professional accounts
 carry a flat monthly subscription fee of $39.99 no matter how many items you sell. This type
 of account makes sense if you're planning to sell a lot of items. An individual account
 is a better choice if you only plan to make a few sales each month. Instead of a flat
 monthly subscription fee, you pay a per item fee of $0.99 for every item you sell. Both
 individual and professional seller accounts are eligible for FBA service. Now let's take
 a look at the features which are only available for professional selling account. Customized
 shipping rates. Bulk listing tools. Advertising, deals and coupons. Automated pricing tools.
 Amazon Selling Coach. Marketplace web services and much more. Now let's look at Step 2. List
 products. You can list a product to sell on Amazon in less than five minutes. Here's how.
 Amazon's product listing allows sellers to match an existing listing. If someone is already
 selling the same product or create a new listing if you are the first or only seller. If you
 are reselling products already present in Amazon's catalog, this can be a fast way to add selection
 to your account. What if my selection doesn't match the existing offers? If you are selling
 your own brand, private label, or bringing new product selections to a category, you'll
 want to create a new detail page on Amazon. To create a new listing, you will need a product
 identifier, also known as a barcode. Commonly used identifier terms include UPC, GTIN, EAN
 or ISBN. Not exactly sure what a barcode is. Look for barcodes on some of your favorite
 items or watch what a clerk scans to get a price in a store. Look at the numbers printed
 below the barcode. A 12-digit number is a UPC or a 13-digit number is an EAN. If the
 products you intend to sell do not have a barcode. Apply for a GTIN exemption. You can
 then add the selection without the need for barcode numbers. Add listings to your account,
 one by one using the add a product option. Add bulk inventory all at once by using the
 add products via upload option. Special listing options cater to the unique demands
 of handmade and customizable listing sellers. Step three, fulfill orders and provide outstanding
 customer service. Now that your product listings are complete, choose how to best fulfill orders.
 The FBA program gives sellers the opportunity to use Amazon's world-class shipping, logistics
 and customer service partnerships to benefit their buyers. Sellers using FBA send their
 products to Amazon's fulfillment centers. We take care of packaging, shipping, returns
 and customer service issues. Products are prime enabled with a prime badge logo. Sellers
 that prefer to do their own or merchant fulfilled shipping are responsible for packages being
 shipped and delivered on time, customer service and returns. Step four, receive payments from
 your sales proceeds. Amazon pays you through an automatic clearing house payment to your
 bank account every two weeks on eligible sales proceeds. To determine sales proceeds and
 profitability, sellers should become familiar with Amazon's fee schedule. On your Seller
 Central home page, type in selling on Amazon fee schedule in the search bar. This will direct
 you to the help page. The help page lists all fees related to selling on Amazon. Lastly,
 we recommend you to visit account health under the performance tab on seller central to regularly
 monitor your performance as a seller. This wraps up how selling on Amazon works. Thank
  you, and happy selling!
