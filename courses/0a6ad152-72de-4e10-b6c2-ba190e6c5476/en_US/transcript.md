# Sponsored Products advanced features and campaign optimization strategies

Let's explore some advanced features and optimization strategies that can help you improve
 your campaign performance. After watching this video, you'll be able to enhance your campaigns
 through several key features. You'll learn to apply rule-based bidding, implement scheduled
 bid rules for specific timeframes, make bid adjustments for placements, leverage Amazon
 built audiences for targeted bid boosts, and set up budget rules to manage campaign
 spend effectively. In the advertising console, select a specific campaign within the campaign
 manager, you can then apply advanced features on the campaign settings page or during campaign
 creation. Let's begin with rule-based bidding, an automated strategy that reduces manual
 bid adjustments, return on ad spend, or ROAS measures the revenue generated for every
 dollar spent on advertising. Once you set your ROAS parameters, Amazon automatically
 adjusts your bids, which can increase up to three times your base bid during high opportunity
 periods. For example, you could set your ROAS to three as a guardrail within the campaign
 settings. Now that you understand rule-based bidding, let's explore scheduled bid rules.
 This feature allows you to automatically increase bids for specific timeframes, helping you
 focus on peak shopping hours and events. For example, you could apply a rule to automatically
 increase your bids by 10 percent on Saturday evenings between 5 and 9 p.m. when you've
 noticed higher conversion rates. Another way to refine your bidding strategy is through
 adjusting bids by placement. This allows you to tailor your bids to help improve visibility
 in high converting placements. You can increase bids for top-of-search placements and adjust
 bids for product pages. For example, consider a 20 percent top-of-search bid adjustment
 and monitor performance. To help further enhance your campaign performance, let's look at bid
 boosting for Amazon-built audiences. This feature enhances your targeting with audience
 bid adjustments. These adjustments apply to audiences such as those with a high likelihood
 of purchase based on recent shopping activity, previous brand purchasers who bought your
 product in the last three months, and brand product considers who clicked or added to
 cart without purchasing. Consider starting with a 10 to 20 percent bid increase for these
 audiences and measure results over two weeks before further adjustments.
 Finally, let's explore budget rules for managing your campaign spend effectively. Schedule-based
 budget rules allow you to automatically increase budgets for specific timeframes, particularly
 useful during seasonal peaks and promotional periods. For example, you can set your budget
 to increase by 30 percent for Black Friday. Performance-based rules help you capitalize
 on well-performing campaigns by automatically adjusting budgets based on key metrics. These
 metrics include advertising cost of sale, ACoS, which measures your ad spend relative
 to sales. Click-through rate, which shows how often customers click your ads when they
 see them, and conversion rate, which indicates how often customers purchase after clicking
 your ads. These advanced features work together to optimize your campaigns. Start by testing
 individual features on a few campaigns, monitor results for at least two weeks, and gradually
 expand successful optimizations that align with your business objectives. Apply advanced
 features in the advertising console.
