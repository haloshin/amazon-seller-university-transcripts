# How to use the Sponsored Brands video ad format

Video can instantly help your ad stand out, so let's look at how to use the sponsored Brands video ad format.
 When you're setting up your campaign, pick your ad format as video, you'll pick one product to advertise, and go down to the creative section.
 Add a short video that showcases your product in action.
 You can see video requirements and guidance to help pass moderation right from here.
 Once your video finishes uploading, you'll see a preview of your ad on the right, and it's time to move on to the rest of your campaign.
