# Resolve Brand Name Approval error

(gentle music)
 When adding a new product to the Amazon store,
 you may encounter a brand name approval error.
 This appears when you enter a brand name
 that hasn't been approved by Amazon.
 We require all brand names to be approved before listing
 to ensure only authentic products are listed
 in the Amazon store and intellectual property rights
 of brand and product owners are protected.
 If you sell a product without a brand name or logo,
 you can avoid the brand name approval error
 by entering generic as the brand name
 or selecting this product does not have a brand name.
 Generic listings don't have access
 to brand selling benefits,
 and other sellers can suggest updates
 to the product detail page.
 Once listed as generic, a product's brand name
 cannot be updated.
 To associate a brand name later,
 you will have to create a new listing.
 If you are a brand owner,
 you can resolve the brand name approval in two ways.
 Either by enrolling in Amazon brand registry
 or requesting approval for an unregistered brand name.
 Enrolling your brand in Amazon brand registry
 lets you use your brand name in your listings
 and unlocks access to selling benefits
 like creating your storefront, A+ content,
 and sponsored brands.
 You get greater control over your product detail pages
 and access to automated protections,
 programs, and reporting options.
 To enroll your brand, you will need your brand name
 or logo that includes your brand name,
 permanently affixed to your products or packaging.
 You also need an active registered trademark
 for your brand name or logo
 or a pending trademark application.
 If you meet these requirements,
 visit the Amazon brand registry page
 to review country specific requirements
 and enroll your brand.
 After approval, you can list products
 using your registered brand name in Seller Central.
 Alternatively, you can request approval for a brand
 that isn't enrolled in Amazon brand registry.
 This option does not require a trademark
 as long as the brand name or logo
 is permanently affixed to your products or packaging.
 Complete the Apply to Sell application
 by clicking the button in the error message.
 Upload a minimum of two images
 showing your brand name or logo on your products
 or packaging and confirm they meet all requirements.
 Add your contact information and submit the application.
 Note that if you initially listed products
 using an unregistered brand name
 and later enroll in Amazon brand registry,
 you'll need to contact Seller Support
 to ensure your newly registered brand name
 is properly synced with your existing listings.
 To monitor your application status in Seller Central,
 open the main menu, hover over Catalog
 and click View Selling Applications.
 If you encounter this error while uploading products in bulk,
 reach out to Seller Support by hovering over Help
 located in the top right corner of Seller Central
 and selecting Get Help and Resources.
 Select Add a Product as the issue type.
 Then choose received an error code or message
 and error 5661, 5665 brand name authorization.
 Select Create a Case and provide the batch ID
 of the inventory file processing report
 and other required information.
 If your application is approved,
 you can begin listing products under your brand name.
 For more information,
 review the brand name approval requirements
 and issue resolution help page in Seller Central.
 (upbeat music)
