# Intro to Seller Central

Seller Central is where you will manage a majority
 of your day-to-day operations when selling on Amazon
 and a place to find resources to learn and grow as a seller.
 Every time you log in, your homepage will provide you
 with a snapshot of what is happening with your business.
 Access the manage and create menus,
 which will help you navigate to all the tools
 Seller Central has to offer.
 Click Settings to access your account settings
 and information, or click the letter icon
 to view the Buyer Seller Messaging or Inbox page
 where you will communicate with your buyers.
 At the top of the homepage,
 you'll see your account health status.
 You can view a breakdown of your account health
 by clicking on the status.
 Your homepage has several workspaces,
 each designed around key actions you take,
 like adding products, managing orders,
 and tracking your payments.
 Each workspace brings the most important information
 and actions directly to your fingertips.
 For example, the products workspace
 helps you review your product performance
 and conversation rates,
 and lets you create new product listings.
 The Actions panel serves as your personal to-do list,
 guiding you to the next steps in your business,
 like submitting missing account information,
 responding to customer messages,
 or resolving issues with shipments.
 You'll also be able to see recommendations
 in each workspace,
 which are personalized to your business needs
 and help you stay on track.
 You have the ability to customize your workspace
 to match your priorities.
 You can drag widgets to rearrange them,
 remove cards that aren't relevant,
 or move information between workspaces.
 If you focus primarily on managing fulfillment operations,
 you can prioritize your inventory and shipping widgets
 to appear at the top of your view.
 You can also access resources like seller forums,
 which provides information and advice
 from other selling partners and Amazon experts.
 And news, which helps you stay informed
 through key updates and announcements.
 Ask questions to selling assistant,
 Amazon's AI-powered chatbot.
 It can share guidance on policies,
 help you locate specific tools,
 and offer recommended next steps.
 Seller University videos will also provide you
 with relevant navigation instructions
 as you continue to learn.
 If you are new to selling on Amazon,
 we encourage you to explore Seller Central
 before getting started.
