# Reaching high intent shoppers with Sponsored Brands and Brand Stores

In this video, you will learn how to guide shoppers to sub-pages in your brand store that match their interests with sponsored brands' product collection and video format.
 Your brand store is more than a landing page. It's a place to inspire shoppers and showcase your product line.
 Let's start by building a sponsored brands' campaign and linking it to a specific sub-page in your store.
 Step 1. Choose product collection or video format and select your target store sub-page, like cappuccino collection.
 Step 2. For videos, spotlight flagship products that are easily found at the top of a sub-page.
 For collections, use lifestyle imagery that brings your products to life, leveraging AI to help create high-quality images easily.
 Step 3. Write a headline that highlights the benefit of your products to communicate value to potential customers.
 Step 4. Choose three products from your sub-page that reflect the theme.
 Try using top performers with strong ratings to maximize impact.
 Step 5. When using keyword targeting, use category terms, feature-specific terms, and branded terms to reach high-intent shoppers.
 For product targeting, select items that are similar to your featured products or aligned with the category and interests related to your sub-page.
 Create separate ad groups or campaigns for each targeting strategy for better control.
 Step 6. Design your brand store sub-page to convert by featuring advertised products prominently at the top of the page.
 Incorporate rich visuals or demonstration videos and consider the opportunity to cross-sell by adding deep links to related categories.
 Linking your ads to specific sub-pages of your store works especially well when promoting specific products within a category,
 launching a new line or targeting high-intent shoppers on a given category.
 This approach is also useful when optimizing your store's performance.
 Use your brand store insights to identify opportunities.
 Check the pages tab to see which sub-pages drive sales and which need more visibility.
 You can then adjust your campaigns to direct more shoppers to underperforming pages or capitalize on your top performing pages.
 Put learning into practice. Create a sponsored brands campaign driving to a sub-page.
 Watch our other two videos in this series to learn more ways to connect sponsored brands to your brand store.
