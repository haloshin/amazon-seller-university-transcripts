# Tips to reduce A-to-z Guarantee Claims and returns

Welcome to our training tips to reduce A to Z guarantee claims and returns. Customers can file
 an A to Z guarantee claim to ask Amazon to investigate an order or product issue when the seller hasn't
 resolved the issue within 48 hours. You can access this information by searching for the Amazon's A to
 Z guarantee claims help page in Seller Central. Most customers don't need to resort to the claims
 process, however. In this training, we'll share tips to help you prevent A to Z guarantee claims
 and returns in the first place. A customer might submit a claim or request a return if the product
 they receive doesn't meet their expectations. Accurate product detail pages help customers make
 informed buying decisions. We recommend you describe your products accurately, use clear images,
 and check that your products are matched to the correct ASINs. In some instances, you might need
 to cancel a customer's order. For example, the customer may have placed an order for a product
 that's out of stock. To maintain the customer's trust, promptly cancel any out of stock orders
 and contact them with an explanation as soon as possible. Then proactively aim to only list
 available products that you can fulfill. Providing excellent customer service can also help reduce
 claims and return requests. A customer might submit a claim or request a return if they've
 messaged you and haven't received a response within 48 hours. They're required to contact you
 before raising a claim, so regularly check your buyer seller messages and respond to customers
 promptly. The buyer seller messaging service is your only approved method for communicating with
 Amazon customers. You can use this tool to respond to messages directly in Seller Central or your
 registered email's inbox. To access the messages page, select the envelope icon in the top right
 corner of the Seller Central home page. You'll use buyer seller messaging to respond to product
 questions or order delivery issues. For order returns or related questions, customers must
 instead contact you through a return request. If a customer mistakenly contacts you about a return
 through buyer seller messaging, instruct them to raise a request using the online return center.
 To respond to a return request, select the main menu icon. Hover over orders and select
 Manage Returns. A customer might submit a claim if they've sent you a return request and haven't
 received a response. Respond to return requests within 48 hours by either approving the request
 or closing it based on the order's return eligibility. Only close a return request if the order is
 ineligible for a return and note that the claim will be granted if you incorrectly close an eligible
 one. If you close a claim for an ineligible order, always let the customer know why their order was
 ineligible. If you approve a return request, provide clear guidelines on how the customer can
 return the product. Your instructions must include a local return address or a prepaid return label.
 For instances where you don't want the product back, refund the customer and let them know they
 don't need to return it. If you'd like to update your return settings, select the gear icon in
 the top right corner of Seller Central and select Return Settings. Fulfill your orders with care
 using adequate packaging and trackable shipping methods. Tracked shipments allow customers to see
 the status of their delivery without needing to contact you first. We recommend you also add
 insurance or request a signature for high-value orders based on your business judgment or when
 Amazon requests it. The valid tracking rate or VTR metric represents the total percentage of
 packages with a valid tracking number during a given 30-day period. You'll need to maintain a VTR
 of at least 95% on eligible orders you fulfill yourself to continue self-fulfilling orders
 without restrictions. To help meet this target, provide the name of the carrier, type of delivery
 service you're using and tracking ID when you confirm a shipment. Follow these steps to confirm
 or edit tracking information. Select the main menu icon. Hover over orders and choose Manage
 Orders. Enter the order ID into the search field and provide the revised tracking number. If you'd
 like to monitor your VTR and other shipping performance metrics, select the main menu icon,
 hover over Performance and choose Account Health. Review the shipping performance section of the
 Account Health page. One way to help earn customers trust and reduce claims is to follow through on
 your timing commitments. In other words, confirm order shipments before the ship by date and
 ensure delivery is completed before the estimated delivery date. If a customer's delivery is delayed
 for any reason, notify them through buyer seller messaging and verify that they agree to accept
 a late delivery. As a reminder, an order is considered shipped when the courier first scans the package,
 not when the order's shipping label is printed. Finally, we recommend using buy shipping services
 or buy shipping for order delivery. With buy shipping, you can purchase shipping labels
 individually or in bulk and ship, confirm and track outgoing orders. Because these orders are
 delivered using a trusted network of shipping carriers, you're protected against A to Z guarantee
 claims if the claims are related to delivery issues and you ship the orders on time. To access
 buy shipping, select the main menu icon, hover over orders and choose Manage Orders. Find the
 order you want to ship and select the buy shipping button that appears next to it. Search Use Buy
 Shipping Services in Seller Central to learn more about buy shipping and availability. Buy shipping
 also helps ensure customers can return their orders without delay. In certain marketplaces,
 we'll authorize returns that align with Amazon's return policy and provide customers with prepaid
 return shipping labels on your behalf. You can use SAFE-T claims, also known as seller assurance
 for e-commerce transactions claims, to appeal our decision to issue a refund to a customer.
 To qualify for a SAFE-T claim, we must have already fully refunded the customer.
 To review a list of possible reasons you may be eligible for reimbursement,
 search for reimbursement policy for prepaid return labels in Seller Central. If you'd like
 to file a SAFE-T claim, select the main menu icon, hover over orders and choose Manage SAFE-T
 Claims. Select File a New SAFE-T Claim. Enter the order ID for the return and click Check Eligibility.
 If the order is eligible, we'll ask you to select the reason that best describes the case for your
 reimbursement claim and to attach supporting documentation. For example, you might attach proof
 that we initiated a return for an item that was outside of the return window. After you submit
 the SAFE-T claim, we'll email you to let you know if we've received it. Aside from this
 notification, any discussion regarding the claim will take place on the Manage SAFE-T Claims page.
 You now have the tools to help minimize claims and returns and provide a great experience to
 your customers. Thank you and happy selling in the Amazon store!
