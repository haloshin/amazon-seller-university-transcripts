# How to set up your Brand Store on Amazon

Brand Stores are customizable multi-page storefronts, where you can showcase your products and create unique shopping experiences in the Amazon Store.
 This video shows you how to build an effective brand store, from designing your home page to creating product-rich sub-pages using available layout tools and tiles.
 Let's start by creating your home page.
 Add your brand name and logo, then enable Display All Deals page to automatically show your live deals on a page next to the home page.
 It will only appear when you have four or more active deals.
 Next, choose how your products will appear.
 We recommend selecting the standard layout, unless you're working with larger product images like fashion or home decor.
 Finally, give your home page a description and pick a template to get started building your store.
 Now, craft your home page.
 Start with an eye-catching hero image, the main banner at the top of each page, to showcase your brand story and products.
 Add an image with text overlay for your main slogan or value statement.
 Create category tiles linking to product pages, showing your full offering at a glance.
 Use a product grid or collection to highlight new arrivals or seasonal products.
 Consider adding video or shoppable images to help boost engagement.
 Add a section displaying best-selling or recommended products, which updates automatically.
 When adding a new page, select a template and customize it with tiles featuring products, images and text.
 You can copy existing pages or tiles using the three dots menu and even move tiles between pages.
 For product tiles, select Hide Out of Stock products to automatically remove unavailable items.
 Remember, pages closer to the home page get more visibility.
 So prioritize what matters most.
 Mobile users access all pages through the categories button.
 Finally, if you run promotions, use the Automated All Deals page we mentioned.
 Additionally, you can add the Featured Deals widget to your home page or sub pages for increased visibility.
 This widget automatically displays your active deals and only appears when you have live deals running.
 Preview your brand store on mobile and desktop, then submit for publishing.
 The moderation process takes up to 72 hours.
 With your brand store set up, you're ready to bring your brand to life and start creating engaging shopping experiences.
 Create your store today.
