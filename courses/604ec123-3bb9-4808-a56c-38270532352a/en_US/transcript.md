# Why Sellers should be spending on Amazon Ads

You can't grow on Amazon without ads. Honestly, that's not a bad thing.
 I am Pallavi Pande, owner and founder at Dtocs Tableware Brand from Portland, Oregon.
 I am an Amazon seller and I spend thousands of dollars on ads across U.S. and Canada. And guess what? We have 10x return on our ad spend.
Here's the biggest insight. Ads are not just for running sales, they're your fastest way
 to learn with data.
 Ads actually tell you what your customers are searching for, what keywords convert, what images get clicks on, and what actual pricing works.
 Organic growth has limits, but ads unlock the data. Think of it as a simple funnel.
 First, awareness. Your product shows through discovery ads. Second, clicks your keyword plus targeting brings traffic. Third, conversion. Your listing
 plus A+ content close the sale. And fourth, retention. Your brand builds repeat
 customers. At every step, you're collecting insights that you can use to improve your
 entire business. Here's what I wish I knew earlier. Don't underfund ads and expect results.
 A budget isn't just spend. It's data. And data leads to smart decisions. So yes, Amazon
 ads are not optional. And that's exactly why they're powerful. Because the faster you
 learn, the faster you grow.
