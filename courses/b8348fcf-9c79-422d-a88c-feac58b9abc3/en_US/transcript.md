# Move your Amazon Warehousing and Distribution (AWD) inventory

The Move Inventory workflow allows you to manage and distribute your Amazon
 warehousing and distribution or AWD inventory. You have the option to choose
 SKUs to be auto replenished to FBA when your FBA inventory gets low. When auto
 replenishment isn't enough to meet expected sales, you can request additional
 inventory be sent to FBA. You can also send your AWD inventory in bulk to your
 own warehouse, third-party warehouses, wholesalers, and distributors with AWD's
 multi-channel distribution capability. To initiate a move, select Move Inventory
 on the AWD program page. If you are moving within the Amazon network, select
 FBA replenishment under Distribution Type. If you are moving to off Amazon
 locations on the Move From Storage page, select Off Amazon Distribution under
 Distribution Type. Then select the origin warehouse from the move from drop-down
 and the destination from the destination drop-down. To add channels and
 destinations, click Manage Destinations when selecting the Off Amazon
 Distribution option in the Move Inventory workflow. Then enter the channel and
 destination information, then click Save. Next, select the SKUs you want to move
 by entering the number of boxes to move. Click Confirm to confirm the move and
 then click Review Move Request. On the Review Move Request page, confirm the
 move request by clicking Confirm Move Request. Once you see the confirmation
 page, your move request has been completed.
