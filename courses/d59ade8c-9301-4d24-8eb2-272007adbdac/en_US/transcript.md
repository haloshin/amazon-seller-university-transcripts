# This $2 Amazon coupon reversed a sales dip — here's what Happy Start did

Hi, I'm Jerry from The Happy Start. As a small business owner, one of the most frustrating
 feelings is knowing that you have a great product, but it's just sitting there, getting
 buried in the search results. Today, I want to talk to you about how coupons and promotions
 helped us stand out. Amazon offers several promotional tools, and one of the most powerful
 ones for us is the coupon. It's simple. You set a discount like 5% off or $2 off, and
 the customer clicks a little box to clip it before they buy. In the past, we've used
 the promotional coupons for three main things, product launches, reviving slow sales, and
 the holidays. For example, when we saw a random sales dip a few months ago, we didn't panic.
 Instead, we turned on a simple $2 off coupon, and that promotion made us stand out amongst
 all the other listings. Our click-through rate jumped almost immediately, and our sales
 velocity eventually recovered. In short, coupons can catch the scroll and the eye of
 the customer in a way a normal price tag just can't. Here's a pro tip. Know your psychology.
 If your item is under $20, use a percentage off promotion instead of a dollar amount. It
 feels like a bigger deal to the customer. If it's over $20, use a dollar amount, since
 the customer will perceive that as real cash in their pocket. But be careful. You don't
 want to stack discounts by accident, or you might end up selling your product for free.
 Use coupons and promotions wisely and watch your sales grow.
