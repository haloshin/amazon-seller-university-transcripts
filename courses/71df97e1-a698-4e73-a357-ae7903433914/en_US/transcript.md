# Intro to Seller University

Seller University offers a library of resources
 and trainings to help you navigate selling on Amazon
 and grow your business no matter your level of experience.
 To find learning content in Seller Central,
 search for Seller University or select the Learn How to Sell
 on Amazon recommendation card from the side menu.
 You can bookmark the page for easy access.
 From the Seller University main page,
 you'll see a wide range of topics
 designed to support you throughout your selling journey.
 As a beginner seller,
 start with foundational material,
 overviewing topics like fulfillment,
 listings, brand registry, and more.
 When you're ready to dive in deeper,
 look for step-by-step guides
 that walk you through Seller Central workflows
 like sending shipments to Amazon fulfillment centers
 or creating promotions.
 As an experienced Amazon seller,
 find more advanced trainings on business growth strategies,
 such as optimizing your listings,
 pricing products more competitively,
 discovering new selling tools, and more.
 Regardless of your experience level,
 you can access courses which are a series of trainings
 designed to help you complete an action
 such as enrolling in fulfillment by Amazon.
 To help you learn as you work,
 you may notice relevant Seller University content
 integrated throughout Seller Central.
 Click the learn more or watch tutorial buttons
 for access to task-specific guidance
 without leaving your current workflow.
 Some of our Seller University videos
 can also be found on the Amazon Seller University
 YouTube channel.
 This is a great place to start
 if you don't have access to your Seller Central account yet,
 but want to get a jump start on learning.
 When you have questions, need help,
 or are looking for ways to grow,
 Seller University is here to support you.
 Content is regularly updated
 to reflect new features and best practices.
 So check back frequently to stay informed
 about the latest selling opportunities on Amazon.
