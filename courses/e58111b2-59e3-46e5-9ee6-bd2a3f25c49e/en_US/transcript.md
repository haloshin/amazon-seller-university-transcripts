# Address a Restricted Products policy violation

In this video, we'll cover one, actions Amazon takes to address restricted
 products policy violations, two, addressing restricted products policy
 violations, and three, best practices to remain compliant. Note that this module
 was developed specifically for sellers operating in the US and provides an
 overview of US policies. Policies in other countries would vary. Refer to the
 restricted products help pages in your local seller central account. Restricted
 products policies are put in place to protect Amazon's customers from products
 that are illegal, illegally marketed, unsafe, offensive, or prohibited by Amazon's
 policies. These policies also give customers confidence that all products
 available for purchase in the Amazon store are compliant with local laws and
 regulations. Further, within the US there could be state-specific guidelines. For
 example, some products are subject to additional regulation in the state of
 California. Make sure to check that your listings comply with all policies for
 each country where you sell. Let's first review actions Amazon takes against
 restricted product policy violations. Amazon constantly works with regulators,
 third-party experts, and selling partners to improve and innovate on behalf of
 customers and ensure that any listings that violate applicable laws or
 regulations or Amazon's restricted product policies are dealt with seriously. If any
 listing is in violation with Amazon's policies we will take corrective action
 as appropriate which may include immediately removing the listing from
 your catalog, suspending or terminating your selling privileges, destroying
 inventory in our fulfillment centers without reimbursement, returning
 inventory, terminating the business relationship and permanent withholding
 of payments. Further, the sale of any illegal or unsafe products could result
 in legal action. Please ensure that all of your listings are compliant with these
 policies and local laws and regulations. Please also note that Amazon updates our
 restricted products policies and help pages to reflect continuously changing
 laws and regulations. We attempt to notify our selling partners in advance of
 these changes via email and when applicable via the Seller Central News
 widget, newsletter, and forums. When possible, we proactively notify selling
 partners with clear expectations of the time period to sell through their
 inventory before removing the detail pages. In some cases, Amazon needs to take
 immediate action and our help pages are updated with revised policies. Hence, it
 is always a good practice to frequently review the policy pages relevant to
 your products to ensure that you are in compliance. If you discover listings that
 are in violation with our policies or applicable laws, you can contact us. Please
 visit the category, product, and listing restrictions help page to find more
 information and relevant links on how you can report a violation. Based on the
 type of violation, you may need to provide information to facilitate an
 investigation. This may include the store or business name of the seller you are
 reporting, the ASIN, relevant marketplaces where the violation is taking place, an
 explanation detailing the violation and any other supporting documentation. You
 will also be required to provide your email and contact number. Next, let's
 review how to respond to a restricted products policy violation notice. If you
 receive a notification that your ASIN or product was removed due to a
 restricted products violation, please read the full performance notification and
 check the policy help pages to understand why your ASIN or product might have been
 removed. Depending on the nature of the notice, you can take several actions to
 review, resolve, and reduce your future risk of account deactivation. The
 performance notifications page can be found under the performance tab in the
 seller central main menu. This page shows many of the account deactivation and
 listing level violation enforcements and messaging. Selling partners could also
 receive notifications via other channels such as email. If you believe that your
 product or ASIN was removed in error, you may appeal using the account health
 dashboard. To do this, hover over the performance tab in the seller central main
 menu, then select account health. Removed ASINs can be reviewed and appealed under
 product policy compliance. Before you contact seller support, please ensure
 that you have followed all the instructions in your restricted products
 performance notification and have provided all of the documentation and/or
 evidence requested. Make sure to respond to individual ASIN level
 violations in a timely manner to help prevent account deactivation. To access
 more details on this topic, visit the additional information and resources for
 sellers about our restricted products policy help page. Here are some helpful
 tips for selling partners to ensure that they remain compliant with local laws
 and Amazon policies. Keep product pages about the product and ensure that the
 detail pages are accurate. Carefully review our restricted products policy
 help pages and your product catalog on a regular basis to ensure that your
 products are compliant. Don't forget about products included in inventory
 feeds or those that are uploaded using an automated system. Check the news widget
 and seller forums periodically for any notified changes. Also make sure to check
 your messages under performance notifications for any specific policy
 related changes. A product may still not be permitted even if it is not
 expressly listed on our restricted products policy help page. Assign someone
 to carefully review each product you are listing to ensure it is compliant. When
 buying inventory from another source apart from the manufacturer, make sure to
 understand the sourcing and origin of the product and take care to understand the
 legal framework in which the product or products may be sold. If selling
 supplements or eligible drugs confirm that the ingredients are compliant not
 just with our policies but with FDA regulations and guidelines. The FDA
 orange book on prescription and OTC drugs and the FDA green book on animal
 drugs are helpful sources. Make sure to also check the list of recalls, market
 withdrawals and safety alerts on the FDA website. Sellers participating in the
 fulfillment by Amazon or FBA program should comply with additional FBA specific
 product restrictions in addition to Amazon's restricted products policies.
 These include hazardous materials and substances regulated by the Narcotics
 Act. For instance the sale of vehicle tires and alcoholic beverages are
 prohibited for sale by FBA.
