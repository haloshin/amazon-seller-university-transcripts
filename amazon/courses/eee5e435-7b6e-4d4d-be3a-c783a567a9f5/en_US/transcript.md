# 3 Essential Tips Every New Amazon Seller Needs to Know

Hey, I'm Michael Koka, head of Digital Commerce at Revolution Nutrition.
 I scaled our Amazon channel from 0 to tens of millions in annual revenue in just 2 years.
 Here are 3 things every seller should understand early on.
 First, treat your listings like your storefronts.
 Don't rush it.
 Invest in content, images and messaging from day 1 because that's what turns traffic
 into sales.
 A weak listing will waste ad spend and slow momentum.
 Second, let data guide  your decisions.
 Pay attention to conversion rate, search terms and customer feedback.
 Avoid building strategies around opinions or assumptions.
 Amazon gives you a lot of insights if you actually use it.
 Third, be patient and consistent.
 Amazon is not overnight success, momentum builds over months not days.
 Small improvements made weekly compound fast over time.
 Focus on getting the fundamentals right, availability, content quality, pricing and customer experience.
 Those basics matter more than any shortcut.
 If you stay focused on customers and keep learning, Amazon can become an incredibly
 powerful growth channel.
