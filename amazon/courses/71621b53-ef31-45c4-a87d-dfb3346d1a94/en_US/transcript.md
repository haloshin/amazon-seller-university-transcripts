# Intro to Amazon Buy Shipping

Amazon Buy Shipping is a tool available to sellers
 who handle fulfillment of their own products.
 This is called Fulfilled by Merchant or FBM.
 When you receive an order and prepare to ship it
 to your customer, you have a few options.
 You can ship your order with a carrier of your choice
 and confirm that the product is on its way
 to your customer by providing a tracking ID,
 or you can use Amazon Buy Shipping.
 Amazon Buy Shipping lets you purchase
 and print shipping labels
 and automatically confirms and tracks shipping
 in Seller Central, as well as some third-party tools.
 Amazon takes care of your shipping details
 and provides recommendations for shipping services
 in our network of trusted carriers
 that are highly likely to deliver on time,
 fit your preferences, and offer the lowest available cost.
 This can help you provide your customers
 with the fast, reliable shipping they love.
 Amazon Buy Shipping also helps safeguard
 your performance metrics, which impact your account health.
 Good account health is essential
 to remaining an active seller in the Amazon store.
 When selecting your carrier with Amazon Buy Shipping,
 you'll see the option to purchase shipping labels
 marked as OTDR protected.
 OTDR, or on-time delivery rate,
 measures how often your products are delivered
 by the promised delivery date you set.
 By using a shipping label that is OTDR protected,
 your on-time delivery rate will not be impacted
 even if your shipment arrives late.
 Note that you will need to have shipping settings automation
 and automated handling time on the shipping settings page
 enabled to take advantage of OTDR protected labels.
 Learn more about these tools
 by searching Shipping Settings Automation
 and Automated Handling Time in Seller Central.
 Amazon Buy Shipping can also protect you
 against A to Z guarantee claims related to delivery issues.
 We will cover the cost of these claims
 and stop them from impacting your OTDR
 if you purchased a shipping label marked as claims protected.
 Making sure all your shipments have valid tracking numbers
 is another important step that Amazon Buy Shipping helps
 support.
 We automatically upload tracking information
 for all orders that use Amazon Buy Shipping,
 which helps you maintain your valid tracking rate or VTR
 and makes it easy for you and your customer
 to see where an order is in the delivery process.
 To learn how to use Amazon Buy Shipping for your next order,
 search using Amazon Buy Shipping for seller-fulfilled orders
 on Seller Central.
