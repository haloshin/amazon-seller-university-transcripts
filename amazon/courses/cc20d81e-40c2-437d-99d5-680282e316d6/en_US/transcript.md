# Benefits of enrolling in Amazon Brand Registry

(upbeat music)
 Amazon Brand Registry can help you protect
 and grow your brand in the Amazon store.
 To participate, you'll need to have a registered
 or pending trademark and your brand name or logo
 permanently affixed to your products or packaging.
 Once enrolled, you gain increased control
 over your product detail pages.
 Your registered trademark establishes legal rights
 while Amazon's technology actively monitors
 for counterfeits and IP violations.
 Brand Registry offers multi-layered protection
 with powerful tools and programs to safeguard your brand.
 As a rights owner for your brand,
 you can report infringement through report a violation,
 prevent counterfeits with the transparency program,
 remove unauthorized listings with Project Zero,
 and partner with Amazon's counterfeit crimes unit
 to take legal action against unauthorized sellers.
 You'll also have opportunities to optimize your listings
 and expand your brand's presence
 with tools like A+ content,
 as well as gain valuable insights
 from customer reviews and programs like Amazon Vine.
 Brand Registry can help you grow your customer base
 through multiple channels,
 create targeted advertising campaigns
 with sponsored brands and sponsored display ads,
 engage shoppers with dynamic content
 through posts and Amazon live streams,
 and build repeat business with subscribe
 and save delivery discounts.
 Track and optimize your performance
 with powerful analytics tools like brand analytics
 or test different versions of your content
 with manage your experiments
 to discover what resonates best with customers
 and drives growth.
 The more tools you use,
 the stronger your brand presence on the Amazon store
 can become.
 Not sure you're ready for Brand Registry?
 Review our Prepare to Enroll in Brand Registry checklist
 to find out how you can work toward becoming eligible.
 Visit the Brand Registry website
 to explore all the benefits of the program
 and begin enrollment.
 (upbeat music)
