Staff management, adding a delivery associate.
 Log in to Seller Central and select Service Settings from the Settings menu.
 Under the Staff Management tab, select Add Staff.
 Enter the name and contact details for the delivery associate and add a photo.
 Select if they perform jobs and/or if they assign jobs.
 You can also select which coverage areas they can see orders in.
 After entering all relevant details, select Save.
 Back on the Staff Management tab, you will see your new staff member
 and can monitor background check status for all your delivery associates.
