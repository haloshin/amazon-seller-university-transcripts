# How to detect downward sales trends

(upbeat music)
Hi everyone! As new sellers, we would love to check our sales all the time, right? It's great if it's always going upward, but what if we see a sales dip? Today I'm going to talk about a checklist that you can go through quickly to self-diagnose any sales downward trend.
 First, I would go to the Account Health page to see if there is any policy warning, violation, or compliance issues that you have to deal with to get your listing reactivated. The dashboard
 is very self-explanatory and you should be able to spot the issues right away. Second
 place I will see is pricing health under pricing on Amazon. It will tell you if there's any
 of your ASINs loses buy box due to pricing issues. And third place I will look at would
 be the inactive listing tab under manage all inventory. It will show you all the listings
 that are not active due to common reason out of stock or softer listing suppression
 issues such as any problems with your title, any problems with your images that cause
 listing suppressions. The fourth place, what if your PPC is not running due to payment
 failure? That's the fourth place I will look. If there is any payment issue with your PPC,
 it will show up on top of the PPC console page as a red banner and tell you that your
 PPC is currently paused because the payment doesn't go through. Now the second part would
 be softer reasons why you see a sudden sales dip, including your active running coupon
 or your active running Lightning Deal or best deal. Are there any errors with them? If there
 is any error, you are going to see a need attention badge underneath that particular coupon or
 that particular deal so that you can go in quickly and fix it. Usually the common reason
 for deal or coupon suppression is also pricing issues. And then the promotions that are important
 to you, I would suggest that you go and check in and see if those promotions are expired
 without you knowing it or they're running out of budget already, which is also a reason
 why they're not running. The last thing I would check is the best seller rank. It's
 on top of Amazon.com page. You can check for your niche is your ASIN ranking well relative
 to other competitors. I actually check that daily to make sure my ASIN is well positioned
 within my niche. So those are all the checklists that we should quickly look. Happy selling!
