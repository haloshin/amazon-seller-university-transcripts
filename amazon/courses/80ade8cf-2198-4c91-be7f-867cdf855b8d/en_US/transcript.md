# How to get started with contextual targeting in your Sponsored Display campaign

Let's see how to get started with sponsored display contextual targeting to target your ads
 against products and categories. First, we recommend starting with 10 or more well-performing products.
 Then choose contextual targeting. You can add entire categories to your targeting or individual
 products. You can add or refine our suggested categories. Search for ones yourself and include
 a dynamic segment of products similar to the ones you're advertising. To target individual products,
 use our suggestions, search, enter a list, or upload one.
