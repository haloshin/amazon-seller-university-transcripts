# Best practices for creating brand names and logos

Welcome to our video best practices for creating brand names and logos. Brand
 names and logos can be powerful tools for sellers hoping to attract loyal
 customers. They help customers recognize and remember products and
 distinguish one brand's goods from another's. If you're a new brand in the
 process of creating a brand name or logo, the most important thing to remember is
 that it should represent your products. Brand names and logos come in all shapes
 and sizes. There's no one way to create them but think carefully about the
 products you sell and why customers might want to buy them before designing
 your brand name or logo. Ask what makes your products unique, valuable and
 memorable. You'll also want to keep these best practices in mind. Stay simple. The
 best brand names are often short, easy to pronounce and straightforward to spell.
 Make it memorable. Your brand name and logo should stand out and be easy to
 recognize and recall. Stay descriptive. Coming up with highly creative names is
 fun but if they're too abstract or complicated they can prevent customers
 from easily understanding your brand and products. If you sell a particular
 product you might want to include it in your name. If your goods are of a certain
 quality or type choose language or images that make that clear. Create a brand name
 or logo that helps customers attach meaning and value to your products. For
 example, if your products are eco-friendly consider associated words and images.
 Finally, remember that if you're planning to sell your products in the
 Amazon store using a logo it needs to include your brand name. Logos and
 brand names also need to be permanently affixed to your products or packaging.
 These requirements help Amazon pair brands with the right products. There's
 also a couple of things to avoid while creating your brand name or logo. You
 don't want to use offensive or culturally inappropriate words or images. Be sure
 you also understand what the words and images in your brand name and logo mean
 to a variety of audiences especially if you're translating text to another
 language. And avoid simply translating a brand name literally from one language
 to another. Brand names are culturally specific and translating them word for
 word often means losing or changing the original meaning. You also don't want to
 create a name or logo that's too similar to a competitor's. That won't help
 distinguish your products from theirs and it could lead to claims of trademark
 infringement or other legal or intellectual property issues. Your brand
 name and logo can use wordplay or newly invented words. They can evoke a feeling
 or emotion. You can also stick to describing who you are and what you
 produce. However you choose to represent your products, take the time to brainstorm
 and collect feedback on your brand name or logo. It can be a good idea to consult
 a brand agency or other expert too. This concludes our best practices video.
 Thank you and happy selling in the Amazon store!
