# AI Creative Solutions Prompting Best Practices

Looking to enhance your ad creatives with AI?
 Amazon Ads offers a range of AI-powered tools designed to help you produce high-quality visuals quickly and easily.
 Whether you're just getting started or refining your creative strategy, understanding how to guide these tools is key.
 In this video, we'll cover five key tips to help you improve your prompts and create high-quality visuals with Amazon Ads' AI creative solutions.
 By the end, you'll know how to guide our AI-powered tools to more effectively generate quality,
 on-brand creative that supports your advertising goals.
 First, be clear and specific with your prompts.
 Instead of a broad idea, use detailed and rich language that captures your desired subject, style, mood, and colors.
 Instead of using a generic prompt like "a dog," use a golden retriever puppy, playing in a sunny park, captured in soft morning light.
 Second, include context and brand elements. Add any brand cues or campaign themes to guide the AI's output, example prompt, an urban coffee shop in the early morning, featuring my brand's coffee cups next to an espresso machine.
 Third, leverage references and adjust parameters.
 Amazon's tools let you upload reference creative or choose from built-in themes or styles, allowing greater control over the creative output.
 Upload reference images to maintain consistent color palettes or subject positioning, ensuring visual harmony.
 Fourth, don't be afraid to experiment.
 AI is a creative partner.
 Try out different prompt variations, themes, and apply edits to see what resonates.
 Keep a log of the prompts and cues that produce the best outputs for your product and brand, making it easier to replicate success over time.
 Finally, review and save or submit your newly generated creative. Carefully evaluate the outputs generated to make sure they meet your standards.
 Save to your Amazon Ads Creative Asset Library for later use on your sponsored ad campaigns and brand store.
 By following these five best practices, you'll be on your way to creating stunning, on-brand visuals with Amazon's AI creative tools.
