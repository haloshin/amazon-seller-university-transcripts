# Register a trademark through IP Accelerator

Amazon's Intellectual Property, IP Accelerator, connects you with a vetted network of legal professionals
 who specialize in trademark registration. These providers are listed in Amazon's Service Provider Network
 and can guide you through every step of your brand's trademark registration. The network
 includes law firms across multiple global regions. Securing a trademark legally registers your brand
 name or logo with a designated government office. It establishes you as the verified owner and
 provides legal rights and protection for your brand name and logo as intellectual property.
 Having a trademark is one of the basic requirements for enrolling in Amazon brand registry,
 which offers several benefits for selling in the Amazon store. You can start your Amazon brand
 registry enrollment using your pending trademark application number even before the trademark is
 fully approved. This gives you faster access to brand registry benefits and brand protection tools.
 To access IP Accelerator providers, search for Explore Services in Seller Central.
 Adjust the drop-down menus to your business location. Where do you want to sell?
 Select IP Accelerator as your service type and click Search to refresh your results.
 Use the filters on the left side to narrow your provider's options based on your specific needs.
 Review available providers, their services, expertise and client reviews. When you identify
 a suitable provider, click the Contact Provider button to submit your service request. Providers
 typically respond within two business days with initial information and next steps for your trademark
 application process. Beyond trademark filing, some providers also provide assistance with the
 patent and copyright application, mediation for IP disputes, holistic brand protection strategies,
 and trademark searches for pending applications or registrations similar to your brand name.
 Ready to protect your brand? Start your trademark journey with IP Accelerator
 and gain access to brand registry benefits sooner.
