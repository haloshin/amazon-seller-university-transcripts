# Master AI tools for Amazon listings: Quick optimization guide

I'm Lizz, the creator of PO' UP! Card Game,
 which are party games celebrating black experiences
 from college to the workplace.
 I've been an Amazon Seller for a couple of years,
 and today I'm going to share a few tips
 for optimizing your listing on Amazon.
 First things first, you want to make sure
 that you have high quality product photos
 ready to go for your listing.
 And this should include a mix of product photos,
 which are images of your product against a white backdrop
 at different angles.
 And this should also include a list of lifestyle photos,
 which are images of people interacting with your product,
 or images of your product in a usual setting
 where people can find your product.
 So for example, my card game is a party game.
 So my lifestyle images include people
 around a table playing the card game.
 And I also have some images,
 including the dimensions of the product,
 and a few tips and ideas for where this product can be used.
 What I also recommend for listing optimization
 is looking at two to three competitor listings on Amazon,
 copying their listings into an AI tool,
 and asking this AI tool to help you develop
 your own listing using keywords for SEO optimization.
 And then what you're going to do
 is you're going to copy the words
 in that listing into your AI tool from your competitors.
 From there, you're going to ask your AI tool
 to create a listing for your product,
 leveraging some of the key phrases and tips
 used in your competitors listing
 that should be leveraged in your own listing.
 And don't forget to ask your AI tool
 to make sure that your listing is optimized for SEO,
 meaning that people are able to leverage keywords
 to find your product on Amazon
 when they're looking for maybe similar products
 or looking for products in that category.
 You want to make sure that your listing pops up,
 and this is a great way to not have to develop
 your listing from scratch
 and to make sure that you are standing out
 amongst your competitors.
 And last but not least for listing optimization,
 I recommend leveraging your A+ content on Amazon.
 A+ content is what you see when you scroll
 to the bottom of the listing
 that provides more dynamic images explaining a product
 in a way that you may not have been able to explain
 at the top of a page, which is limited
 to a certain number of words or characters.
 Definitely take advantage of the A+ content,
 add videos to your page where possible,
 and just really make sure that your brand messaging,
 but also your brand look and feel,
 comes across by the time people scroll
 to that portion of your listing.
 I hope you found these tips helpful
 for listing optimization on Amazon.
