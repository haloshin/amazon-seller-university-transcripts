As a new seller, you'll need to provide tax information
 to begin selling on Amazon.
 Amazon uses this information to generate required tax forms
 for your region and make accurate tax calculations
 on your fees and transactions.
 Without proper documentation,
 you may face trouble in receiving your payments.
 To get started, take the self-service tax interview
 if you haven't already.
 You can find it by going to Settings, clicking Account Info,
 and selecting Tax Information and Tax Information.
 In this form, you'll verify your identity
 and whether you're operating as an individual or business.
 You'll also provide residency and citizenship information
 along with tax identity details.
 If you're unsure about any form field,
 click the Learn link or the information icon
 for specific guidance.
 Double-check all your inputs as misspellings
 or incorrect numbers may cause the validation
 of your form to fail.
 As your business grows, keep your tax information updated
 on Seller Central.
 Remember, your VAT GST sales taxes are determined
 by your location and the Amazon Product Tax Code
 of your product category.
 Take time now to verify your tax information is up to date.
