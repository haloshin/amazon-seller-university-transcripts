# Generate an inventory report

Welcome to our training on how to generate inventory reports in Seller Central. All sellers with a professional selling account have access to a number of inventory reports for their products in the Amazon Store. In this video, we'll show you how to access and customize yours. You can use inventory reports to see your current quantity levels, SKU-level performance, SKUs that are suppressed from Seller Search results, inactive or have quality warnings. To access your inventory reports, open the Seller Central main menu, hover over Reports,
 and select Inventory Reports. Use the Select Report Type drop-down menu to choose the report
 you'd like to generate. Below each type, you'll find a summary of what's included in the
 selected report. Once you've chosen a report, click customize the columns for this report.
 A new page will appear and display additional metrics. Select a metric from the available attributes
 and click Add to customize the report with that metric. To remove the metric, simply select
 it from selected attributes and click Remove. Select Save when you're ready to save your
 custom report settings or click Reset to reinstate the default settings. Then return to the Inventory
 Reports page and select Request Report to generate your customized report. If you'd like to
 request a report without making any changes, simply click Request Report after choosing
 the report type. Reports are typically generated within 45 minutes, so keep in mind that your
 inventory may change during this interval. Use Reports to spot-check your inventory and
 identify trends and compare them against your own records. You'll find your reports in the
 report status and download columns on the Inventory Reports page. After the report is
 generated, the report status column shows ready. To download a report, click the download link
 next to it. Search Inventory Report in Seller Central to find examples of the metrics included
 in each report. You can also find more information about how to manage and update your inventory
 by watching the Managing Your Inventory video in Seller University. This concludes our
 training on how to generate inventory reports. Thank you and happy selling in the Amazon
 Store!
