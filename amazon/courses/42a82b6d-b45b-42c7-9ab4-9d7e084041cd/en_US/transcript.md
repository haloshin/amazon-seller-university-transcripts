# Leafael Jewelry uses Creator Connections strategy for sales growth

Hi everyone, today I would like to talk about Creator Connections, an easy to use tool that's designed
 to generate additional sales for you. It's under Amazon's advertising and brand content.
 There are a few easy steps to set it up. The first tip I want to give is the length of
 the campaign. I would usually start a campaign for at least 3 to 6 months long because that
 tells the creators that they actually have enough time to submit content and enough time
 for them to generate sales for you. The second would be the budget. Honestly, I would really
 suggest maximize your budget number. It can go up to crazy number. Usually, I only ended
 up using a fraction of that budget, but again, that sends a very positive signal to creators
 that you are in Creator Connections for a long haul and you have enough budget for your
 campaign. And then the third would be commission rate. I would suggest starting from 10% at
 least. Depending on how competitive your niche is, say my niche is super competitive. Sometimes
 I go up to 20% as a commission rate. And then fourth would be the campaign brief. The information
 in there would often be referred by the creator in their videos. So they're important, but
 don't make it longer than a paragraph because anything longer than that, a lot of creators
 they would just tune out. And then when creators messages you for samples, I usually ask my
 VA to ask them for links of at least one of their social media platforms so that we can
 check engagement. Not just the number of followers they have, but how many comments, how many
 likes, and how often do they post on their platform? Those are all metrics for us to
 determine if this creator is a good fit for us. And once they submitted the content and
 generate some sales for you, definitely, definitely generate a list of effective creators who
 has converted for you and who has submitted good content that fits your brand, you could
 see it from the dashboard what kind of content they submitted. There is a link to that. So
 there's really a lot of information you can see to determine how a creator can help you
 generate sales the next time when you have a new campaign, definitely send messages to
 invite those effective creators first and really prioritize them in terms of communicating
 and sending samples. So that's my list of tips for Creator Connections. Happy selling.
