 Dynamic bids up and down, adjust bids up or down based on conversion likelihood. Allow at least two to three weeks before making bid adjustments.
 Best practices include analyzing conversion rates across placements, increasing bids for high-converting placements, and regularly monitoring and updating for optimal performance.

 Common optimization mistakes to avoid, making too many changes at once, not allowing sufficient time for results, and setting unrealistic performance targets.
