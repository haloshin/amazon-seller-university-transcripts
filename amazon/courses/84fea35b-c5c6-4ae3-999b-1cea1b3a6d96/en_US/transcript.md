# Overview of Amazon selling policies

When you sell in the Amazon store,
 you must comply with all applicable laws
 as well as Amazon's policies.
 Our policies are in place for the protection
 of both our customers and sellers.
 Let's take a quick look at a few of those policies,
 product condition, authenticity, customer reviews,
 and listing policies.
 Ensure that your product listings follow our requirements
 and that the products your customers receive
 match the listing information on the product detail pages.
 These policies help us provide customers
 with the best possible experience
 when they shop in the Amazon store.
 Our product safety and restricted products policies
 ensure that products are safe for customers
 and that they comply with all legal regulations.
 When we identify products with a high safety risk,
 we either restrict the sale of the product
 or take extra precautions to ensure the product is safe.
 The Amazon Intellectual Property Policy
 protects the rights of intellectual property owners,
 products listed in the Amazon store,
 must not infringe on the rights of intellectual property owners,
 including copyrights, patents, and trademarks.
 These are just a few of the selling policies
 you will encounter as an Amazon seller.
 You can find details about all of our selling policies
 by searching for selling policies
 and seller code of conduct in Seller Central.
 Sellers who don't comply with our policies
 will be notified on the account health dashboard,
 which you can find by clicking on your account health rating
 from the Seller Central home page.
 Failure to comply with any of our selling policies
 may impact your ability to sell in the Amazon store.
