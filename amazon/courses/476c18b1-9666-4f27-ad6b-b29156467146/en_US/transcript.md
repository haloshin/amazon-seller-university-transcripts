# Getting started with Sponsored Brands

Welcome to Sponsored Brands. In this video, you'll learn how to create and manage effective advertising campaigns that help increase your brand visibility on Amazon. Sponsored Brands are ads featuring your brand logo, a custom headline, a lifestyle image or video, and multiple products. These ads appear in prominent shopping placements, helping you increase visibility and direct shoppers to your Amazon Brand Store or product detail pages. They support brand discovery and consideration during the customer journey.
 Let's begin with Ad Placements. Sponsored Brands can appear in three key locations on Amazon. At the top of shopping results, where they immediately catch shopper attention.
 Within shopping results, mixed with organic listings to maintain visibility as shoppers scroll, and on product detail pages where they can influence decisions closer to the point of purchase.
 Understanding where your ads appear helps you anticipate shopper behavior.
 To set up a Sponsored Brands campaign, go to the Amazon Ads console. Start by naming your campaign to keep things organized.
 Then, set your start date and assign a daily budget. This budget determines how often your ad appears each day.
 If your daily budget runs out, your ads will pause until the next day, potentially missing, valuable traffic.
 Keep in mind, budgets reset at midnight.
 Next, choose a campaign goal. There are two Sponsored Brands objectives.
 Drive page visits, directs shoppers to specific product detail pages, or your Amazon Store.
 This helps increase engagement with your brand.
 Grow Brand Impression Share focuses on gaining visibility, especially at the top of shopping results to support brand recognition and reach.
 Choosing the right goal helps Amazon match your ad with the best settings, including format, targeting, and bid strategy.
 Once you've chosen your goal, you can apply bid adjustments to support it.
 These adjustments help you prioritize certain placements or audiences.
 For example, increasing your bid for top of shopping results helps maximize visibility in premium positions.
 If you want to reach new audiences, boost your bid for new to brand shoppers, people who haven't purchased from you in the past year.
 These options give you more control over how your budget is spent and can improve performance in key areas.
 Now, let's choose an ad format. Sponsored Brands offers three options.
 Video ads play automatically in shopping results.
 They're great for showcasing product use or telling your brand story.
 Product collection ads display up to three items side by side.
 This is ideal for featuring bestsellers or complementary products.
 Store Spotlight Ads highlight multiple store pages, allowing you to direct shoppers to different product categories within your brand.
 Each format is designed to align with specific campaign goals and shopper behaviors.
 To help your brand reach the right audience, you can choose between two targeting strategies.
 Keyword targeting matches your ad to relevant shopping queries.
 Use broad phrase or exact match types to control reach.
 Enable theme targeting to simplify setup.
 Amazon's machine learning suggests relevant keywords based on shopper signals.
 Product targeting places your ad next to related or complementary items.
 This helps you reach shoppers already browsing in your category.
 Choosing the right targeting method ensures your ads are relevant and timely.
 After launch, use campaign metrics to evaluate performance and make improvements.
 Track your click-through rate to see how often shoppers engage.
 Monitor impression share at the top of search to assess visibility and review new to brand sales to understand how many new customers your ads are reaching.
 These metrics help you refine your strategy and improve results over time.
 Sponsored brands give you tools to grow your presence and connect with shoppers on Amazon.
 To begin, log in to the Amazon Ads console and create your first sponsored brands campaign.
