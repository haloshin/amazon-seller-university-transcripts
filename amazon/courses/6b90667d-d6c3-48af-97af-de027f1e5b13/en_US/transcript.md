# How to improve your Brand Store design

In this video, we'll show you how to refresh your brand store with rich visuals and interactive experiences.
 Let's start with the imagery.
 First, customize your hero section. This is the image displayed at the top of every page.
 Use images that represent your brand values or specific product categories.
 You can upload your own or use Shutterstock's free library.
 You can look for images that represent seasonality, your values, or your target audience.
 Next, add image tiles.
 You can use image with text for easier navigation by linking to category-specific sub-pages,
 or image tiles without text to showcase lifestyle imagery.
 Now, let's create interactive shopping experiences with shoppable images.
 These allow customers to explore products in context and add them directly to their baskets.
 To add them, select "Shoppable Image" when adding a new section.
 Feature bestsellers, complementary products, or new arrivals. Ensure product circles are visible and placed strategically.
 Finally, incorporate videos to tell your story and show products in action.
 You have three options, background videos, which autoplay without sound, and are ideal for brand awareness.
 Video tiles, which play on click, perfect for deeper storytelling.
 And video reveal, which is an 8-second interactive format for highlighting top products.
 Remember to keep videos concise and mobile-friendly.
 With these visual and interactive elements, you can drive sales and build customer loyalty.
 Ready to start?
 Log in to the Amazon Ads console and start building your next-level brand store today.
