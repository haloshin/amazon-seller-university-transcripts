# Amazon Product Listing Optimization: What actually works for Revolution Nutrition

Hey, I'm Michael Koka, Head of Digital Commerce at Revolution Nutrition.
 I scaled their Amazon channel from zero to tens of millions in annual revenue in just
 two years.
 Everything I'm sharing comes from real execution.
 Let's dive in.
 Today I want to share a few practical listing optimization tips that can help sellers improve
 conversions on Amazon.
 Start by building your listings around customer intent, not internal product language.
 Use real search terms and make sure your content answers questions, shoppers actually have.
 Keep your titles and bullet points clear and benefit driven, don't just list features.
 Explain what the product does for the customer.
 Highlight things like taste, ease of use, performance or convenience, so buyers immediately understand
 the value.
 A powerful step many sellers skip is competitor assessment.
 Look at top performing listings in your category and identify what they do well whether that's
 structure visual or messaging.
 Then find gaps where competitors fall short and use those opportunities to differentiate
 your own listings.
 Images play a huge role in conversion.
 Use visuals that educate and not just display the product.
 Lifestyle shots, ingredient callouts and comparison graphics help shoppers make faster
 decisions.
 Finally, use customer reviews as ongoing feedback, reviews, reveal objections, expectations and
 opportunities to improve your copy and images.
 Treat them like free market research.
 My biggest advice is to treat your listings like a landing page, continuously test, learn
 from competitors and customers and always build around shopper needs.
