# Intro to Fulfillment by Merchant (FBM)

When you list products on Amazon,
 you choose how each item will be fulfilled.
 One option is fulfillment by merchant or FBM.
 With FBM, you manage storage and shipping yourself.
 You can handle fulfillment directly,
 work with a trusted partner,
 or take advantage of Amazon's shipping carrier solutions.
 FBM can be a great fit
 if you prefer to manage your own fulfillment,
 sell large or slow-moving items
 or already have a strong logistics setup in place.
 You can use FBM for your entire catalog
 or combine it with fulfillment by Amazon FBA.
 For example, you might use FBM for items
 that need special handling or custom packaging,
 and FBA for products that benefit
 from Amazon's storage and delivery network.
 You can adjust your approach any time
 as your business grows.
 FBM allows you to sell to customers
 both domestically and internationally,
 giving you control over shipping fees for each region.
 To ensure your shipping rules and fees are applied correctly
 before orders start coming in,
 navigate to settings and update your shipping settings
 using shipping templates.
 Here's how FBM works.
 First, decide where you'll keep your inventory,
 whether that's your own space, a warehouse
 or a fulfillment partner.
 Make sure they're organized and ready to ship quickly.
 When a customer places an order,
 you'll pick, pack and ship the item
 by the promised delivery date.
 Once you ship, customers should obtain their tracking information
 to track their delivery every step of the way.
 As an FBM seller,
 you need to meet Amazon's performance standards.
 This includes maintaining a low-order defect rate,
 shipping orders on time
 and providing valid tracking for your shipments.
 These metrics help ensure customers
 have a reliable shopping experience.
 If you consistently meet these requirements
 and can deliver orders quickly,
 you may qualify for seller-fulfilled prime.
 This program allows your offers to display the prime badge
 without sending your products
 to Amazon's fulfillment network.
 You can choose FBM in two ways.
 The first is during listing creation.
 When you create a new product listing,
 select fulfilled by merchant as your fulfillment method.
 The second way is by updating an existing SKU.
 If your product is already listed,
 you can switch the fulfillment method to FBM
 at any time under your offer settings.
 Once orders start coming in,
 prepare shipping labels through the Manage Orders page.
 Amazon offers tools to help you purchase shipping labels
 from major shipping carriers at discounted prices.
 Or you can purchase shipping labels externally
 from the carrier or through other shipping solutions.
 If you purchase shipping labels somewhere else,
 confirm the shipment and provide tracking IDs
 on Seller Central.
 When preparing packages,
 make sure each package can withstand a three-foot drop
 on any side or corner
 to reduce damage-related customer complaints.
 Use the Account Health Dashboard
 and Fulfillment Insights Dashboard
 under the Manage Seller Fulfilled Products page
 to track key metrics like on-time delivery rate,
 valid tracking rate, and cancellation rates.
 Review your Voice of the Customer Dashboard regularly
 for feedback trends.
 You have the option to manage returns
 and customer service yourself or delegate it to Amazon
 by enrolling into Customer Service by Amazon.
 If you are handling returns and customer service,
 track your returns on the Manage Returns page
 and respond to all customer messages
 within 24 hours to maintain customer satisfaction.
 FBM gives you control, flexibility,
 and the ability to tailor fulfillment to your business
 by managing inventory, shipping,
 and returns effectively.
 You can provide a great experience for customers
 and grow your Amazon business with confidence.
 Visit Seller University for other resources
 on FBM Strategies and Best Practices.
