# Send inventory to Amazon Warehousing and Distribution (AWD)

(upbeat music)
 Let's create your first Amazon
 warehousing and distribution, or AWD shipment.
 From the AWD program page, click Send to AWD.
 First, you'll add inventory to send
 by selecting from your available SKUs
 or choosing File Upload.
 Then you'll choose your ship from address
 and preferred ship to region.
 A single shipment can contain up to 150 unique SKUs,
 but you can send multiple cartons for the same SKU.
 If you plan to ship single SKU pallets
 to the west coast palletizable region,
 you need to use single SKU pallet labels on your pallets.
 Select the single SKU pallet template
 under Packing Details in step one,
 choose Inventory to Send.
 Next, you'll choose either Amazon partnered carrier
 or seller managed carrier as your shipping method.
 Regardless of the method, you'll then print
 the required serialized ship container code
 or SSCC box labels.
 Each label is unique for one box
 and the labels must be placed flat and uncovered
 on the outside of each box to avoid delays or rejections.
 If you are sending pallets to AWD,
 you will continue on to step four
 to print your pallet labels.
 Each pallet must have a pallet label
 and every box on the pallet must still have
 the serialized ship container code, SSCC label on it.
 Four copies of each label will be generated
 for each pallet in your shipment.
 Place one copy of the label on each
 of the four sides of the pallet
 in the center near the top
 on the outside of the stretch wrap.
 Lastly, make sure to verify
 that your SKU details and box count in your shipment,
 match the pallet and box label information.
 Now you're ready to ship your products
 to an AWD distribution center.
 After your shipment is on its way,
 you can track the status of your shipment
 by going to the program page and clicking Track Shipments.
 View the tracking history
 on the Amazon Distribution Center Shipments tab.
 (upbeat music)
